Our Laboratories

Spaces for experimentation, practice and project work

DSA Lab — Data Structures & Algorithms

The DSA Lab provides students with a practical environment to develop programming and problem-solving skills. Students learn to implement and analyze important data structures and algorithms and understand their applications in solving computational problems.

Learning Outcome: Students develop efficient programming techniques, logical thinking, and the ability to design optimized solutions for real-world problems.
